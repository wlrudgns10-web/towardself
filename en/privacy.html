Privacy Policy

The Whisperling Test ("the site") values your privacy and follows applicable data protection laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Your test answers

Your answers and results are calculated only inside your browser. They are never sent to a server, and the site operator cannot see your answers or results. When you close the page, your answers are gone.

2. Information we collect and why

TypeWhat is collectedPurposeHow long it's kept
Email inquiriesYour email address, name (if given), messageReading and replying to your messageOne year after replying
Collected automaticallyServer access logs such as IP address, browser information and access timeRunning the service, security, troubleshootingAccording to the hosting provider's retention policy
Visit statisticsPages visited, where visits come from, device and browser type, approximate region, etc. (Google Analytics; nothing that identifies you, such as your name)Counting visitors, improving the siteUp to 14 months

The site has no sign-up and collects no personal information beyond the items above.

3. Service providers

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA): website hosting (GitHub Pages)
  • Google LLC (USA): web fonts (Google Fonts), visit statistics (Google Analytics), advertising (Google AdSense)

These providers have servers outside Korea, so information may be transferred abroad when you use the site.

4. Cookies and advertising

The site uses Google Analytics for visit statistics and Google AdSense to show ads, so Google cookies may be stored in your browser. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • How Google uses advertising cookies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.
  • You can opt out of visit statistics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can block cookies in your browser settings. The test still works if you do.

5. Your rights

You can ask to view, correct or delete the personal information you sent us by email. Send a request through the contact page and we will handle it promptly.

6. Children

The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

7. Privacy contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact page.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.

Effective date: November 11, 2026
