Whisperling Test

The little devil between you and other people

A Whisperling lives at everyone's ear. When someone speaks to you, it quietly twists their words and whispers, "They're ignoring you," or "You messed up again." Then we end up hearing the Whisperling's version instead of what the person actually said. How often is your Whisperling chiming in right now?

Please read first This test is not a clinical diagnostic tool. It is a self-understanding test that has not yet been validated. It is a homemade scale inspired by Carl Jung's theory of the shadow and Phil Stutz and Barry Michels's idea of "Part X" (the inner enemy), and the practices in your results draw on Viktor Frankl's logotherapy and Stutz and Michels's The Tools. It has not been studied for reliability or validity, and it is an unofficial test with no connection to these authors. Use your results only as prompts for self-reflection, and if you're struggling, please talk with a professional.
