How to Read Your Results

Your scores aren't a verdict on you. They're questions that help you look at yourself.

Your Whisperling type

In the 8 scenario questions, whenever you chose an answer that followed the whisper rather than the other person's actual words, we count which trick that answer used.

  • Thorn: makes it sound like the other person is attacking or ignoring you (Jung: shadow projection)
  • Grader: makes it sound like the other person sees you as not good enough (the inner critic)
  • Halo: makes the other person sound special and perfect (Jung: anima/animus projection)
  • Dozing Whisperling: when you didn't choose any whisper-following answers

Your result card also shows, for each type, You may also have and Worth watching for. No type ranks above another; each is simply a different way of hearing people.

Because each trick appears in a different number of answer options, your type is the trick with the highest ratio of answers chosen to options available. Each trick is explained further in Who Is the Whisperling? and Projection.

Whisperling Volume

This overall score averages the five detailed measures after turning them all in the direction of "the Whisperling chimes in more." The higher it is, the louder the whisper sounds compared with what the other person actually said.

  • 0–30 Soft: you tend to tell apart what people actually said from the whisper.
  • 31–60 Medium: usually fine, but with certain people or situations the Whisperling gets louder.
  • 61–100 Loud: your inner story is big and vivid, so the whisper may sound louder than what people actually say.

This number is the Whisperling's volume, not yours. Loud isn't bad and soft isn't good. The Whisperling also gets louder when you're especially sensitive, worn out lately, or carrying old hurts.

For Reading the situation and Self-awareness, higher scores mean you're less pushed around by the Whisperling. For the other three, lower scores mean that. Each measure is converted to 0–100 and placed in one of three ranges:

  • 0–33 Low
  • 34–66 Medium
  • 67–100 High

How to use your results

  1. Read about your Whisperling's trick and think about whether you've heard that kind of whisper recently.
  2. Try the "tool for your type" and "practice for you" from your results page for one week.
  3. Take the test again a few weeks later and see what's changed. Results can also shift with your mood on the day.

Limits of this test

The Whisperling Test is a homemade scale built with reference to Carl Jung's theory and the ideas of Phil Stutz and Barry Michels. It is an unofficial test with no connection to those authors. It has not been studied for reliability (whether the items consistently measure the same thing) or validity (whether it measures what it intends to), and there are no norms to compare against. The score ranges and interpretations are also based on theory. Please use your results only as prompts for self-reflection.
